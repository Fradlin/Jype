## Keyboard layouts
* Avro Phonetic, Khipro: implemented inside Riti (MPL-2.0).
* Probhat: `third_party/riti/data/Probhat.json`, shipped in the Riti repository (MPL-2.0).
* National (Jatiya), Munir Optima, Borno, Avro Easy: copied from the OpenBangla Keyboard
  repository (https://github.com/OpenBangla/OpenBangla-Keyboard, **GPL-3.0**), with the
  preview images removed. They are only compiled in when the `extra-layouts` Cargo feature
  is enabled (it is on by default). **Before distributing the .exe to other people, either
  confirm you are comfortable with that licence or build without them:**
  `cargo build --release -p jype --no-default-features`
